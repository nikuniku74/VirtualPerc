**Description**

```
A percussionist that listens to your band and plays along.

Virtual Percussionist follows the music's tempo and adds shaker, congas, clap, tambourine and triangle to your mix. Analysis happens on your device, in real time, without reading future audio.

THREE WAYS TO LISTEN
MIXER: the most precise choice for live use. Connect a class-compliant USB audio interface or mixer with an input and an output. IN selects the channel to follow.
IPAD: the microphone hears the room. Handy for practice, but more sensitive to noise and distance. If I HEAR MYSELF appears, turn the percussion down or move the device away from the speakers.
TRACK: load a file and tap PLAY, including with AirPods. With no file loaded, PLAY starts the built-in demo song.

GET STARTED
1. Choose the input in Settings.
2. Set MIC within the fuchsia band.
3. Tap START: with FOLLOW on and START NOW off, the percussion waits for a stable lock.
4. Choose a style or AUTO, then set swing, energy, reverb and levels. STOP silences the part.

YOU STAY IN CONTROL
FOLLOW tracks changes in the music. FIXED plays at the BPM you set. TAP lets you tap the tempo; ÷2 and ×2 correct the rhythmic level. If the one is off, tap THE 1 IS HERE on the downbeat.
START NOW starts the part immediately at the last known or default tempo, even if provisional. PAUSE IF OFF holds back an already unreliable entry and fades out a part that stays in red.
METRO selects 4/4 or 3/4. The guide is always in Settings.

LIMITS TO KNOW
An audible rhythm section is needed. Solo piano, voice, rubato, weak or noisy inputs may cause a slow or inaccurate lock. Tempo may be read at half or double speed; the downbeat may need manual correction.
In a long recording, if the next song stays at the previous tempo after silence, tap STOP and then START.
Set tempos from 50 to 200 BPM. 6/8 and 12/8 are not recognised. A shorter buffer reduces delay but increases the risk of clicks: raise it if needed.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. Audio is never recorded, uploaded, shared or exported. With valid access, playback can continue when the screen locks. For iPhone, iPad and Apple silicon Macs (iPad version).

PRO ACCESS
Using the app requires an active trial, a monthly or yearly subscription, or a lifetime purchase with no renewal. A 7-day free trial is available to eligible new subscribers. Payment is charged to your Apple ID at confirmation or when the trial ends. Subscriptions renew automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple ID > Subscriptions. Restore purchases is on the access page.
Terms of Use: https://www.apple.com/legal/internet-services/itunes/dev/stdeula/
Privacy: https://virtualpercussionist.it/privacy/
```

Un percussionista che ascolta la tua band e suona insieme a lei.

Virtual Percussionist segue il tempo della musica e aggiunge shaker, conga, clap, tamburello e triangolo al tuo mix. L'analisi avviene sul dispositivo, in tempo reale, senza lettura anticipata dell'audio.

TRE MODI DI ASCOLTARE
MIXER: la scelta più precisa per il live. Collega un mixer o un'interfaccia audio USB class-compliant con ingresso e uscita. IN sceglie il canale da cui seguire il tempo.
IPAD: il microfono ascolta la stanza. Comodo per provare, ma più sensibile a rumore e distanza. Se compare SENTO ME STESSO, abbassa le percussioni o allontana il dispositivo dagli altoparlanti.

Tempi impostabili da 50 a 200 BPM. Il 6/8 e il 12/8 non sono riconosciuti. Un buffer più corto riduce il ritardo ma aumenta il rischio di scatti: alzalo se necessario.

## Note per la revisione

```
Virtual Percussionist is a live percussion companion: it listens to a band (microphone, line input from a mixer, or a track loaded in the app) and plays shaker, congas and other percussion in time.

For real use, MIXER mode is the most precise (the app receives the band's signal and sends the percussion out at the same time, through an audio interface or mixer); the demo below needs none of that.

To try it without a band: activate a sandbox trial or restore valid test access, then open Settings and tap PLAY in the INPUT section. With nothing loaded, PLAY loads a built-in demo song (about 35 seconds) and the percussion follows it; tap START on the main screen to hear the part. The microphone is only needed for live use; the demo needs no permission.

The microphone is analysed on the device only; nothing is recorded or sent. Background audio is used so a live set keeps playing when the iPad locks.

Subscription: 7-day free trial, then monthly or yearly (group "Virtual Percussionist Pro"). Without an active trial or purchase, the offer opens at launch and cannot be dismissed. An active user can also open the account page from Settings > PRO. Expiry or revocation stops playback. Restore Purchases, Terms of Use, Privacy Policy and Licenses are on that page. No account is required.
```
